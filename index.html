🎼 Music Theory

🎵 Musical Notes

The basic musical notes are C, D, E, F, G, A and B. These notes repeat in higher and lower octaves.

🎹 Octave

An octave is the distance between one note and another note with the same name at a higher or lower pitch.

🥁 Rhythm

Rhythm controls when musical sounds happen. Common time signatures include 4/4, 3/4 and 6/8.

⏱️ Tempo

Tempo describes the speed of music and is commonly measured in BPM, or beats per minute.

💡 Music Tricks

1️⃣ Practice Slowly

Start at a comfortable speed and increase the tempo gradually.

2️⃣ Use a Metronome

Practicing with a steady beat improves timing and rhythm.

3️⃣ Practice Scales

Scales improve finger control, pitch awareness and familiarity with keys.

4️⃣ Record Yourself

Listening back can help identify timing, pitch and technique problems.

5️⃣ Learn by Ear

Try to identify notes and simple melodies without looking at the instrument.

6️⃣ Keep a Routine

Short daily practice sessions are easier to maintain than occasional long sessions.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What should beginners learn first?

Start with rhythm, basic notes, simple scales and easy songs.

How long should I practice?

Even 20–30 minutes every day can be useful when practice is focused.

Can I learn multiple instruments?

Yes. Build a strong foundation in one instrument while exploring others.

What is BPM?

BPM means beats per minute and is used to describe musical tempo.
